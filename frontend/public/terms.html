Terms and Privacy

Last updated July 2026

Pun & Post is a small, experimental side project — not a polished commercial service. It's real: real payment, real postcards, real mail. But it's built and run by one person for fun, not a company with a support team. Please read this page with that in mind.

What you're buying

You're paying for a single postcard, printed with a joke you chose and a short note you write, mailed via USPS First Class to an address you provide. Delivery typically takes a few business days after printing, though USPS timing isn't something we can guarantee.

If your postcard never arrives (give it a couple of weeks past the typical delivery window before assuming this — mail is sometimes just slow), or if the postcard received doesn't reasonably match the preview you approved before paying, just email us at hi@dadjokepostcards.com to request a refund. We'll handle it case by case and won't make you fight for something reasonable.

Built with

In the spirit of this being an experiment in quickly building something real: Pun & Post is stitched together from a few outside services, each handling one piece of the job.

— Jokes come live from icanhazdadjoke.com, a public joke API. No personal information is sent to it — just a request for a random joke.
— Payment is processed by Stripe. See Stripe's privacy policy for how they handle your payment information.
— Printing and mailing is handled by Lob. See Lob's privacy policy and their data retention policy for exactly how long they keep the recipient's address and other mailpiece data (90 days by default, as of when this was written).

Privacy — what happens to your information

We built this to hold onto as little of your information as possible: our own system has no database, and doesn't store your address, the recipient's address, or your payment details anywhere. That information passes through directly to Stripe (for payment) and Lob (for printing and mailing); see above for links to exactly how each of them handles it on their end.

We don't and won't ever sell, share, or use your information for anything beyond fulfilling the order you placed.

Age

This is intended for adults. If you're under 18, please have a parent or guardian place the order.

Liability

This is a small side project, run without a company or a legal team behind it. It's provided as-is — we'll do our best to make things right if something goes wrong.
